Key Features

Our system uses advanced computer vision technology to ensure driver safety

👁️

Real-time Eye Monitoring

Continuously monitors eye movements to detect signs of drowsiness and fatigue.

🔔

Instant Alerts

Provides immediate audio and visual alerts when signs of drowsiness are detected.

📊

Comprehensive Reporting

Generates detailed reports of driver alertness and potential risk patterns over time.

🔄

Distributed Monitoring

Monitor multiple drivers from a central dashboard with our distributed architecture.

🛡️

Driver Protection

Ensures driver privacy while maintaining effective monitoring capabilities.

📱

Mobile Compatibility

Access monitoring data and alerts from any device with our responsive web interface.
